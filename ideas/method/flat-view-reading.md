# Reading a flat view

**[defined]** A bug that looked like a limit of the method.

The [information of a pose](pose-information.md) is a [geometric mean] of six
eigenvalues. Seen flat, three of them are rounding, around $10^{-17}$, and so is
the mean.

The confirmation, a ratio of two such means, came out at $\pm 10^{150}$.
So the [hold](hold-through-loss.md) was believing noise.

A direction under a millionth of the strongest now counts as a millionth: it is
free, and how free does not matter. A view with all six directions pinned does not
move; half a flat face is worth about half of it, as a test checks.

- **Gear from above**: 47 to 72 of 72, with [no contour term](contour-term.md).
- **Crate, gear and mug scenes**: they now tell their first frames, and start.

**[caution]** A flat face flush on a wall now confirms itself, and rightly: its
hits agree.

So a search is believed only if [its outline stands out](ray-evidence.md) of the
frame on a quarter of its length: an object gone from view is lost, not found on
the wall.

[geometric mean]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_mean
